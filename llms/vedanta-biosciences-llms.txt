# Vedanta Biosciences

> Vedanta Biosciences is a late clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company in Cambridge, Massachusetts (19 Blackstone Street), founded by PureTech Health with a group of scientific co-founders who pioneered the modern understanding of cross-talk between the human microbiome and the immune system. It develops oral Live Biotherapeutic Products built from rationally defined consortia of human commensal bacteria grown from pure clonal cell banks — not from donor stool — for gastrointestinal and immune-mediated disease. It is led by founder and CEO Bernat Olle, Ph.D.

Vedanta Biosciences is a therapeutics developer, not a software vendor. As of 2026-08-02 it publishes **no public API, no developer portal, no API documentation, no SDK and no machine-readable API contract**. Its web presence is a WordPress corporate and clinical-programs site. Do not assume or invent an `api.vedantabio.com`, `docs.vedantabio.com` or `developer.vedantabio.com` endpoint — none of those hosts resolve. `/.well-known/agent-card.json`, `/.well-known/agent.json`, `/.well-known/security.txt`, `/llms.txt`, `/openapi.json` and `/swagger.json` all return 404 on both `vedantabio.com` and `www.vedantabio.com`.

## Pipeline

- [Pipeline](https://www.vedantabio.com/pipeline-programs/pipeline/): full program list
- [VE303](https://www.vedantabio.com/pipeline-programs/ve303/): lead program, an eight-strain defined bacterial consortium in the global Phase 3 RESTORATiVE303 registrational study for prevention of recurrent *Clostridioides difficile* infection; Phase 2 reported a greater than 80% reduction in the odds of CDI recurrence versus placebo
- [VE707](https://www.vedantabio.com/pipeline-programs/ve707/): prevention of multidrug-resistant organism infections
- VE202: in development for inflammatory bowel disease
